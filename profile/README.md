## 🧠 Overview

**Sons of the Forest Trainer** is a Windows PC gameplay utility designed to provide additional control over survival, combat, movement, inventory, crafting, building and world mechanics.

Depending on the supported trainer build, available options can include unlimited health, stamina, hydration, fullness, ammunition, items, strength, movement speed, jump height, building controls, time settings and additional gameplay options.

The trainer is designed primarily for **single-player gameplay** and can be useful for testing survival mechanics, exploring the island, experimenting with different builds and reducing repetitive resource-management tasks.
